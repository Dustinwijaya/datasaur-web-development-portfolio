01 / The assignment

Translate the design.
Keep the experience usable.

The brief called for a responsive Webflow page with a persistent navigation, hero, social proof, product showcase, three-column comparison, video section and footer.

Marketing purpose

The supplied hierarchy introduces the governance problem, demonstrates product capabilities, compares options and offers a route to the demo or sales. Preserving that sequence keeps the page focused.

Delivery constraints

The implementation needed to remain editable in Webflow and work within the free plan. The brief explicitly allowed the video gate to be omitted, with the intended production approach documented.

Assessment project, not a commissioned client engagement or an official Datasaur website. Original brand assets and design belong to their respective owners.

02 / Implementation decisions

The details that made
the page work.

A

Keep the source design recognizable

Used the supplied architectural photograph, Arvo and Geist typography, product artwork and exact page copy. A centered cover crop and 40% dark overlay preserve the hero composition while maintaining text contrast.

The 1920, 1280 and 1024 reference widths informed layout and spacing. Smaller widths were checked in the browser.

B

Make comparison possible on mobile

Fixed column widths pushed information outside narrow viewports. Below 992 px, the table uses 52% / 24% / 24% columns, wrapped labels and smaller icons so the category and both options remain visible together.

Category 52%Private AI 24%Public AI 24%

Tradeoff: wrapped labels make the table taller. This is a deliberate interpretation of the mobile reference.

C

Use purposeful, lightweight interaction

The product accordion uses native disclosure elements. The logo strip repeats a four-brand sequence and moves right on a 32-second linear loop: native Webflow Interactions on staging, CSS keyframes in the portable demo.

Decorative logo copies are hidden from assistive technology. Motion is disabled when reduced motion is requested. The continuous animation's lack of pause controls remains an accessibility review item.

D

Respect the free-plan scope

The video poster opens the supplied YouTube video. A production gate would require form validation, success and failure handling, and a lead-management integration; protected content would also require authorization at delivery.

The current demo has no lead-capture backend or protected video gate.

05 / Contribution & reflection

Review the output.
Document the limits.

My contribution

I directed the assessment implementation and reviewed the result against the brief. My feedback drove corrections to the hero, logo visibility and autoplay, mobile table behavior, and submission materials.

Code implementation, asset preparation, Webflow integration and technical validation were supported by AI and automation.

What the work demonstrates

A reviewable landing-page implementation, responsive problem-solving and documented quality checks. It does not establish conversion uplift, SEO growth, revenue impact or independent pixel-perfect certification.

The source design and marketing claims were supplied for the assessment; they are not original research or independently verified product claims.

Remaining production checks
  • Official Figma pixel-diff comparison and further CSS/class cleanup.
  • Physical-device Safari, Firefox and a complete screen-reader audit.
  • Review continuous motion controls and fixed versus sticky navigation behavior.
  • Measure web performance and add consent-aware analytics if required.
  • Validate the Webflow Designer canvas manually; automated checks did not establish anonymous canvas access.
